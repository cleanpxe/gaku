## 1. $\dfrac{d}{dx}\,x^2 e^x$

Product rule: $2x e^x + x^2 e^x = x e^x (2 + x)$.

## 2. $\dfrac{d}{dx}\,\dfrac{x}{x^2+1}$

Quotient rule: $\dfrac{(1)(x^2+1) - x(2x)}{(x^2+1)^2} = \dfrac{1 - x^2}{(x^2+1)^2}$.

## 3. $\dfrac{d}{dx}\,(2x^3 - x)^4$

General power rule: $4(2x^3 - x)^3 (6x^2 - 1)$.

## 4. $\dfrac{d}{dx}\, x\sin(2x)$

Product, then chain on the second factor: $\sin(2x) + x \cdot 2\cos(2x) = \sin(2x) + 2x\cos(2x)$.

## 5. Tangent line to $y = (x^2+3)^{1/2}$ at $x = 1$

$y(1) = 2$. $y' = \frac{1}{2}(x^2+3)^{-1/2}\cdot 2x = \frac{x}{\sqrt{x^2+3}}$, so $y'(1) = \frac{1}{2}$.
Line: $y - 2 = \frac{1}{2}(x - 1)$.
