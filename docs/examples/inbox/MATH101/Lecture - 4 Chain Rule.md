Prof started with y = (3x+1)^2. Expand: 9x^2+6x+1, derivative 18x+6 = 6(3x+1). Point: the 3 is the derivative of the inside.

Then y = (3x+1)^100 — can't expand. Need a rule.

CHAIN RULE. If y = f(u), u = g(x): dy/dx = f'(g(x)) * g'(x)

examples:
- d/dx sin(x^2) = cos(x^2) * 2x
- d/dx e^{5x} = 5e^{5x}
- d/dx sqrt(1-x^2) = 1/2 (1-x^2)^{-1/2} * (-2x) = -x / sqrt(1-x^2)

gear analogy: two gears, 3:1 then 2:1 -> 6:1. that's the multiplication
